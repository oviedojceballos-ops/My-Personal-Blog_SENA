❤️ My Family

The people who make every moment special.

👨‍👩‍👦 Family Time

Family is the most important pillar of my life, and we genuinely enjoy spending quality time together. During the weekends, we often go to local shopping centers to walk around, watch a movie, or share a nice meal.

We also love visiting the Malecón, where we can enjoy beautiful views, feel the fresh breeze, and share unforgettable moments as a family. These simple activities help us relax, have fun, and create wonderful memories together.

🛼 Outdoor Activities and Sports

Besides our leisure walks, one of our favorite activities is doing sports outdoors. I especially love practicing with my 11-year-old son, Benjamín. He is currently learning how to skate, so I am always there to support him, guide him, and help him improve his balance and confidence on the skates.

My wife, Laura, also joins us, making it a perfect family day. Spending time outdoors allows us to exercise, enjoy each other's company, and encourage Benjamín as he learns new skills.

💙 What Family Means to Me

I truly believe that shared experiences, whether simple walks or active sports afternoons, make our family bonds stronger. These moments keep us connected, help us support one another, and bring happiness to our lives.
